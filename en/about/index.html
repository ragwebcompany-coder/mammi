BSc Aesthetician, MSc · CIDESCO Hellas member

Maria Mammi

Four years of university study, a postgraduate specialisation and two hair-removal certifications — in a small centre in Zipari.

Maria Mammi studied aesthetics for four years at European University Cyprus. Not a short course or a few months' certification: a full university degree, with anatomy, dermatology, cosmetology and clinical practice.

She continued with a postgraduate specialisation in Asia Minor Trapezuntian Therapeutics at the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, a direction that brings the logic of holistic care into modern practice. That is also where her work with essential oils connects: she is a dōTERRA member and uses them deliberately in massage and reflexology sessions.

In parallel she specialised in electrolysis and in laser — two fields that complement each other in practice and are rarely found together in the same centre. Laser clears large areas quickly; electrolysis finishes the job where laser cannot, on white and very fine hair.

She is an official member of CIDESCO Hellas, the Greek branch of the international aesthetics certification body, and an active member of the Dodecanese Association of Aestheticians.

BNL operates in Zipari, Kos. It is a small centre, and that is a choice: one appointment at a time, no waiting and no divided attention. The working philosophy is one thing, and we say it at every first visit — analysis first, recommendation second. And if something is not needed, it is not recommended.

The equipment

What is in the room
and why

No device produces the result on its own. The right choice for your skin and consistency across sessions do.

Diagnostics

APOPSIS

An imaging skin-analysis system paired with a magnetic body analyser. It shows what is happening below the surface — hydration, sebum, pores, pigmentation, elasticity — before any treatment is chosen.

Hair removal

Diode laser

Diode hair-removal laser with contact cooling. It covers a wide range of skin phototypes and finer hair than older systems, and is noticeably more comfortable during the session.

Cleansing

Hydrodermabrasion

Cleansing with water and vacuum instead of abrasion: it lifts dead cells and pore content while hydrating at the same time. No irritation, no downtime.

Firming

RF — Radiofrequency

Controlled heating of the deeper layers to trigger collagen production. Used on the face to tighten the contour and on the body alongside contouring protocols.

Circulation

Pressotherapy

Sequential pneumatic compression that moves lymph in the right direction. For fluid retention, heavy legs, and after body treatments so that what was mobilised actually leaves.

Products

Dermagenetic

The professional range we use in the cabin: exosomes, multisomes, retinol ampoules and Metallo Mineral Skincare — natural mineral coverage with no parabens, talc or silicone.
