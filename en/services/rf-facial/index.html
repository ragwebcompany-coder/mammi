RF Facial — Radiofrequency

Contour tightening without surgery, through controlled heating that wakes collagen up.

  • Duration45 min
  • AddressZipari, Kos
22420 69729 Message on Instagram

Collagen is not lost suddenly with age; it declines gradually. The contour of the face softens, skin becomes thinner, and the shadows around the jaw and neck grow more pronounced. It is a slow process, which is exactly why it responds best when you start early.

Radiofrequency heats the deeper layers in a controlled way, to a temperature the body reads as a repair signal. Fibroblasts are activated and produce new collagen and elastin over the following weeks. The skin surface stays intact — which is why there is no recovery time.

The sensation is warm and comfortable, like a hot massage. We suggest a course of six to eight sessions, one a week, then a maintenance session each month. It pairs very well with mesotherapy: RF builds the structure, mesotherapy improves quality and hydration.

What it includes

  • Assessment of elasticity and contour
  • Cleansing and conductive medium
  • RF applied to face, jawline and neck
  • Serum and mask matched to skin type
  • A session and maintenance plan

Frequently asked

RF Facial

At what age does it make sense?

Preventively from around thirty-five, correctively whenever laxity appears. The earlier you start, the fewer sessions you need.

When do results show?

There is an immediate tightening sensation from the first session, but the real result builds over the following six to twelve weeks as new collagen is produced.

Are there contraindications?

Yes — pregnancy, a pacemaker, metal implants in the area, active infection. We always discuss these before the first session.
