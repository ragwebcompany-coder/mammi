Hair removal

Waxing

Quick, clean and affordable — the answer when you are not on a laser course.

  • Duration15–45 min
  • AddressZipari, Kos
22420 69729 Message on Instagram

Waxing remains the most practical answer when you are not on a laser course: it clears the whole area in one visit, the result lasts three to four weeks, and hair grows back finer than after shaving.

We use hypoallergenic waxes, applied at low temperature for sensitive areas. Technique matters more than the wax itself: the right direction, steady skin tension and a fast movement reduce both pain and redness.

Aftercare matters just as much, especially on an island. No sea, pool, sauna or strong sun for twenty-four hours, and regular gentle exfoliation from the third day so hairs do not become ingrown. We give you written instructions at the first visit.

What it includes

  • Low-temperature hypoallergenic wax
  • Face and body, all areas
  • Preparation and soothing aftercare
  • Written guidance against ingrown hairs
  • Can be combined with another treatment

Frequently asked

Waxing

How long does the hair need to be?

About five millimetres, so two to three weeks of growth. Hair that is too short is not gripped and the result is patchy.

Can I wax while having laser?

Not during the course. Waxing removes the root, which is exactly what the laser is targeting. Shave in between instead.

Can I swim afterwards?

Not the same day. The pores are open and salt or chlorine will irritate them. Wait twenty-four hours.
