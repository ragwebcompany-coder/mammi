The equipment

What is in the room
and why

No device produces the result on its own. The right choice for your skin and consistency across sessions do.

Diagnostics

APOPSIS

An imaging skin-analysis system paired with a magnetic body analyser. It shows what is happening below the surface — hydration, sebum, pores, pigmentation, elasticity — before any treatment is chosen.

Hair removal

Diode laser

Diode hair-removal laser with contact cooling. It covers a wide range of skin phototypes and finer hair than older systems, and is noticeably more comfortable during the session.

Cleansing

Hydrodermabrasion

Cleansing with water and vacuum instead of abrasion: it lifts dead cells and pore content while hydrating at the same time. No irritation, no downtime.

Firming

RF — Radiofrequency

Controlled heating of the deeper layers to trigger collagen production. Used on the face to tighten the contour and on the body alongside contouring protocols.

Circulation

Pressotherapy

Sequential pneumatic compression that moves lymph in the right direction. For fluid retention, heavy legs, and after body treatments so that what was mobilised actually leaves.

Products

Dermagenetic

The professional range we use in the cabin: exosomes, multisomes, retinol ampoules and Metallo Mineral Skincare — natural mineral coverage with no parabens, talc or silicone.
