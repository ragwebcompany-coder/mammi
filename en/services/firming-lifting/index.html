Firming, Lifting & Body Contouring

Laxity is not corrected by losing weight — it needs a stimulus for new collagen.

  • Duration60 min
  • AddressZipari, Kos
22420 69729 Message on Instagram

After weight loss, after pregnancy, or simply with the years, body skin loosens. The odd part is that the more weight you lose, the more obvious it becomes — the fat that filled the space has gone, but the skin has not retracted to match.

Firming works by stimulus, not by removal. Radiofrequency heats the dermis in a controlled way and prompts fibroblasts to produce new collagen and elastin. The result is not immediate: it builds over the weeks after the session, which is why a course only makes sense in full.

Body contouring is the next step: instead of reducing everywhere, we work the outline. We target specific areas — waist, buttocks, inner thigh, arms — so that the shape improves rather than just the size. It is almost always combined with lymphatic drainage.

What it includes

  • Elasticity assessment per area
  • Body RF protocol
  • Targeted contour sculpting
  • Lymphatic drainage to close
  • Hydration and movement guidance

Frequently asked

Firming & Lifting

Is it suitable after pregnancy?

Yes, once at least three months have passed since the birth and provided you are not breastfeeding. We discuss this in detail at the first meeting.

How long does the result last?

The new collagen stays. Natural ageing continues, though, so we suggest maintenance sessions every one to two months.

Can I combine it with slimming?

Yes, and that is usually the right order: reduce the volume first, then work on firming so the skin follows.
