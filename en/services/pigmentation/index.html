Brightening & Pigmentation

On an island, melasma and dark spots are not solved without sunscreen — and not in a single session.

  • Duration50 min
  • AddressZipari, Kos
22420 69729 Message on Instagram

Pigmentation is not one condition. Hormonal melasma is one thing, a sun spot built over years of exposure is another, and the shadow left behind after a breakout is a third. Each needs a different approach and has a different timeline.

So we start with the analysis: APOPSIS shows at what depth the pigment sits, which determines both the protocol and what expectations are realistic. A combination of brightening actives, gentle exfoliation and melanin-inhibiting ingredients follows.

The most important part, though, happens outside the cabin. In Kos the sun is strong eleven months of the year, and without daily broad-spectrum sunscreen every brightening treatment comes undone. We say this at the first meeting, because without it there is no point starting.

What it includes

  • Pigment mapping with APOPSIS
  • Gentle exfoliation adapted to the season
  • Brightening and antioxidant actives
  • Retinol ampoule for the evening routine
  • A sun-protection recommendation

Frequently asked

Pigmentation

Can I do brightening in summer?

Yes, with gentler protocols. We keep the intensive ones for autumn and winter, when sun exposure is lower.

Will the melasma disappear completely?

Melasma is controlled, rarely eliminated for good, because it has a hormonal component. The goal is a visible fade and no relapse.

How many sessions are needed?

Usually six to eight over two to three months, then maintenance through the home routine.
