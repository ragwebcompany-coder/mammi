Hair removal

Permanent Hair Removal (Electrolysis)

The only method that destroys hair at its root, whatever its colour.

  • Duration15–45 min
  • AddressZipari, Kos
22420 69729 Message on Instagram

Laser needs pigment to work: it targets melanin in the hair. That is why on white, grey, blonde or very fine hair the result is poor or non-existent, however many sessions are done. It is the most common complaint we hear from clients who have already tried laser elsewhere.

Electrolysis solves exactly that, because it does not depend on colour. A fine sterile probe is inserted into the follicle and destroys it at the root. Hair that has been treated does not return — it is the only method with a genuinely permanent result, recognised as such internationally.

We work hair by hair, so sessions are short and repeated. It is ideal for the face — chin, upper lip, brows — and for whatever is left after a laser course. We use certified equipment and individually set intensity, so the process is as painless as it can be.

What it includes

  • Assessment of hair and area
  • Sterile single-use probe
  • Individually set intensity
  • Soothing aftercare
  • A repetition schedule

Frequently asked

Electrolysis

Is it really permanent?

Yes. A destroyed follicle does not produce hair again. It does take several sessions, because we work hair by hair and across growth phases.

Does it hurt?

There is a pinching sensation, controlled by the intensity setting. On the face, where it is most often used, most clients find it perfectly tolerable.

How is it different from laser?

Laser is fast over large areas but needs dark hair. Electrolysis is slower but works on any colour and is definitive. They are often combined.
